GCC build (arm-none-eabi-gcc)
=============================

Tento projekt uz vie buildovat bez Keilu cez Makefile v root priecinku.

Prerequisites:
- arm-none-eabi-gcc, arm-none-eabi-objcopy, arm-none-eabi-size
- make

Podporene app ciele:
- test_button
- test_RS485
- test_RS485_modbus

Priklady:
- make APP=test_button
- make APP=test_RS485
- make APP=test_RS485_modbus

Vystupy su v priecinku:
- build/<APP>/<APP>.elf
- build/<APP>/<APP>.hex
- build/<APP>/<APP>.bin

Vymazanie build artefaktov:
- make clean
